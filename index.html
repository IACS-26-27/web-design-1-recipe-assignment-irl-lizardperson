Spam Grilled Cheese

Completely terrible for your health, but delicious!

Ingredients

  • Two slices of bread
  • Original Spam
  • One slice of orange cheddar
  • One slice of Monterey Jack
  • 1 tbsp of butter (preferably Kerrygold)

    • Cut six thin (~1/6-inch) pieces from your block of Spam. Put in an ungreased pan and fry on medium heat until crispy. Set aside.

    Get the slices of bread. Put your orange cheddar cheese on one slice, and the Monterey Jack on the other.

    Lay your Spam slices on top of the cheese (three on each side) and close the sandwich.

    Add butter to the pan. Once it's melted, cook the sandwich, flipping when needed, until the cheese is melted and the bread is browned.

    Optional: Use ketchup or pineapple sauce for dipping.
